Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Your videos stay on your device

Aizipy processes videos entirely in your web browser. Your video and audio are never uploaded to our servers.

What we send to our server

To choose the best moments, the text transcript of your video (words and timestamps only) is sent to our server and passed to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate clip suggestions. We do not store transcripts after the suggestion is returned.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the EU/EEA and UK are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under the GDPR, including access to and deletion of personal data. Since we do not store your videos or transcripts, we generally hold no personal data about you. Contact us with any request.
