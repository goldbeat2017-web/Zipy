Frequently asked questions

Is Aizipy free?

Yes. Aizipy is free to use, with no signup and no watermark.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Your video is processed entirely in your browser. Only the text transcript is sent to our AI to pick the best moments.

Which platforms are the shorts for?

Exports are vertical 1080×1920 MP4 files that work on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

Aizipy works with video files and direct video links. For your own YouTube videos, download them from YouTube Studio and drop the file in.

What languages are supported?

The speech AI understands about 100 languages, including English, French, Arabic and Spanish.

Which browser works best?

Chrome or Edge on a computer give the fastest results, using your graphics card when available.
